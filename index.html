Chiang Mai Weather Atlas

Explore today’s forecast around Chiang Mai, or replay a saved weather system from the ridge to the globe. Open the 3D atlas ↗

Start at your house

  1. 1My houseTap the map or paste latitude, longitude.
  2. 2SaveOrbit the terrain around your pin.
  3. 3Scrub timeCompare rain, clouds and wind through time.

Your pin stays in this browser. Current forecast uses the nearest of three public reference cells and shows its distance. Saved replay samples the archived provincial grid. Use Change pin or Forget pin at any time.

Read the landscape

Orbit the ridge

Drag to rotate and scroll or pinch to zoom. Switch Drag mode to Move to slide across the map. At local scale the ruler shows the terrain’s vertical exaggeration.

Find the sunset

Turn on Sun path and choose Face sunset. Move Solar time to watch the Sun meet the terrain horizon. Shadows account for relief. Trees and buildings can change the view on the ground.

Follow a cloud

Compare the low, middle and high cloud bands. Near the basin, cloud envelopes appear in humid pressure layers with modeled cloud cover. Their shapes represent conditions across a model cell.

Track the rain

Check the model cell for the previous hour’s rain. In Saved replay, Hourly rain paints the province. Six-hour rain on the Southeast Asia chart sums a longer interval.

Compare the scales

Mountains and wind

Try 925 hPa for lower air, then 700 hPa above the ridge. Arrows point downwind. Changing pressure can reveal a turn in wind direction with height.

Regional systems

Choose Saved replay, open Southeast Asia, then choose Pressure. Isobars join equal sea-level pressure. Switch to cloud or rain to see where those fields overlap.

Model vs. station

Open Model cells & airport report. Each ICON cell has a fixed center and elevation. Saved replay also gives the timestamp of the airport’s VTCC observation when one was recorded.

Read both clocks

The weather slider steps by one hour. Regional GFS frames are six hours apart. The inspector gives the frame’s valid time and its forecast run. All displayed times use Chiang Mai time.

Sources and credits

When the viewer opens, it requests DWD ICON Global via Open-Meteo for three fixed public locations: Chiang Mai Old City, Doi Suthep and Crazy Horse Buttress. Refresh forecast checks again. The saved November 2026 replay includes regional NOAA GFS charts, provincial ICON samples and VTCC airport observations from NOAA Aviation Weather Center.
